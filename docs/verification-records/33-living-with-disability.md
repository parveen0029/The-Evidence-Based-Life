# Section 33: How to live after being disabled

2026-09-21. The reason was that readers pointed out that the book lacked a section on disability, and asked whether it was necessary to supplement the physical, psychological, and daily life aspects.

First, I checked the existing coverage: there are only four places in the book related to disability, and they are all on the "certificate and money" side - Section 24, Articles 10 and 11 (timing of disability assessment, disability certificate), Section 7, Article 9 (two subsidies), Section 19 (work ability assessment for work-related injuries), Section 17, Articles 7 and 8 (long-term care insurance, long-term bedridden pressure ulcers, it says that the elderly are not disabled). In other words, the book answers "How to prove disability and how much money you can receive", but it does not answer at all "How to live after the certificate is issued." What is missing are rehabilitation, complications, assistive devices, accessibility, employment, education, psychology, caregivers, and guardianship of mental and intellectual disabilities. After confirming with the user, a new section 33 will be opened, and all five blocks will be written, with a total of 21 items.

Positioning: Only write after the disability has occurred. How to prevent disability is in Sections 1 and 13, so we won’t repeat it. The four calibers (mortality, money, time, personal freedom) are run in parallel, and cross-caliber conversions are not performed.

## Rating instructions

- Article 1: B: The three field actions only have clinical consensus and physiological data (level 5). The review itself states that most interventions are only supported by uncontrolled studies. The magnitude of the benefit is "large" - it prevents fatal consequences such as cerebral hemorrhage caused by sustained high blood pressure; the cost is zero, which is combined into "extremely high".
- Article 4 is set to A (prospective cohort, with quantifiable numbers), but marked "controversial": the lower limit of the 95% CI of RR 1.63 is exactly 1.00.
- Article 5: B: The sample is 232 people, the primary endpoint P=.04 just crosses the line, there is no difference in the secondary endpoint, and the population is elderly residents of nursing homes. The same reasons for classification as in Section 17, Item 8 (Pressure Ulcers).
- Article 10 states B: The law only states that "appropriate subsidies shall be provided". There is no unified national figure. The amount and qualifications for subsidy are determined by local governments.
- Articles 17 and 18 are rated A and marked "controversial": the evidence itself is a large RCT and a Cochrane review, and the controversial points are "the extrapolation of stroke evidence to other causes of disability" and "the main result is negative but the spread is the subgroup number."
- Article 12: The amount of income is defined as "small": the law does not provide a figure, and the amount of reduction or exemption in each province ranges from a few hundred to a few thousand yuan.
- The rest of the Chinese regulatory entries are A: the terms can be checked word for word.

## Beneficiary size

The main theme of this section is that oneself or a family member becomes disabled, which falls into the ①② stage. There are three separate markings: the beneficiary of Article 4 (caregiver) is the reader himself; the beneficiary of Articles 9 and 15 (rehabilitation assistance, schooling) is the child, which belongs to the ② category. There are no entries involving files ③④.

## Things not written in

- **Urinary tract infection and bladder management**: Common complications after spinal cord injury, but I can’t find an original source that can be verified word for word and give numbers. I won’t write about it this time, and I won’t use the general statements in the review to make up the numbers.
- **Nursing and respite services**: The "Law on the Protection of Disabled Persons" only has two sentences: "The state encourages and supports social forces to organize nursing and nursing institutions for disabled people" and "nursing institutions must not insult, abuse, and abandon". There is no operable application path and standard. Writing it in is equivalent to giving only one sentence. The Sunshine Home Plan of the China Disabled Persons' Federation is a departmental project and is not included in the policy database.
- **Regulatory Basis for Stem Cell and Other "Cure" Therapies**: "Stem Cell Clinical Research Management Measures" cannot be found in the gov.cn policy database, nhc.gov.cn has 412 on the entire site, and the original text that can be verified verbatim is not available. Article 6 is changed from Articles 16 and 17 of the "Advertising Law", and the criteria that readers can use (see whether it dares to say enough) are more direct.
- **Total number of disabled people in China**: The often quoted figure of 85 million comes from the second national sample survey of persons with disabilities in 2006, which is too long ago. The 2026-07-27 press conference of the State Council Information Office mentioned the "Statistical Bulletin on the Development of Disabled Persons in 2025", "448,000 new certified disabled people will be employed in urban and rural areas nationwide in 2025" and "80% of disabled people in my country live in rural areas", but the full text of the statistical bulletin cannot be found in the policy database. Therefore, the introduction only uses the WHO's global figures and does not include the total number of China.
