## Candidates not accepted and reasons
- Drunk riding of electric vehicles: The standards and precedents for distinguishing electric bicycles from electric motorcycles are only available on the China Courts website (which cannot be opened). When opened, Guiding Case No. 271 is a case of drunk driving with assisted driving and has nothing to do with electric vehicles.
