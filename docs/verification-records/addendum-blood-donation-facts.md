## Rating and selection

- Decision A: The main evidence is two randomized controlled trials (FeAST 1277 people, INTERVAL 45,263 people) plus a million-level cohort, and the numbers can be verified verbatim.
- **Not marked with "controversy"**: Kuopio's study and the following evidence do not constitute two opposing factions. It is the source of this statement and it is an observational study. Ullum used the same group of people to quantify the effect of healthy blood donors (18.6% → 7.5%). FeAST directly made "bloodletting to reduce iron and protect the heart" into a randomized trial and obtained negative results. It is clearer to write this chain in order in the main text than to label it "controversy".
- **The strength of the cold-sensitive paragraph is stated separately**: The human body evidence is only a small sample of 10 people in Beard 1990 (it would be B if listed separately). The text note states that "it is for the mechanism, not the incidence." It is not used to infer how many blood donors are afraid of the cold.
- **Not written**: The incidence rate of vasovagal reactions (sickness, fainting) - this round I did not find any large sample data from China that could be verified verbatim, so I would rather not write it; nor did I write any statement such as "blood donation can lose weight/lower blood pressure", which is not in the original document.
- Beneficiary: The blood was given to a stranger (level 4). According to the rules of the book, the part that can be returned to you must be clearly stated. Therefore, the remark lists the blood fee reduction and exemption in Article 14 of the Blood Donation Law, the direct reduction and exemption for 28 provinces in the 2024 notice, and the "three exemptions".
