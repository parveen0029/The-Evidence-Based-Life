# Verification Record: Quitting Smoking & Alcohol, Study Methods, Procrastination, and Habit Building Concrete Methods

Verification Date: 2026-09-21. Section 2 (32 -> 38 rules), Section 4 (13 -> 18 rules), Section 6 (24 -> 25 rules), Section 23 (13 -> 18 rules), totaling 17 newly added rules.

Origin: User asked: "Should content on self-discipline be added—how to study a topic, how to avoid procrastination? For example, quitting smoking and drinking only state 'requires high willpower,' but provide zero concrete methodologies."

Pre-work deduplication check: Across 574 rules at the time, **39 rules were labeled with 'Willpower=Yes'**, yet not a single rule explained "what to do when willpower is insufficient." Searching the full text yielded zero occurrences of "varenicline," "nicotine," "naltrexone," "quitline," "spaced repetition," or "active recall." Section 4 already included implementation intentions (Rule 1), exit criteria, breaking down tasks, and self-set deadlines, but lacked an overarching framework and several other evidence-based practices; all 13 rules in Section 23 answered "what to learn" with none answering "how to learn."

User-selected plan: **Disperse across the four existing sections rather than opening a new section** (the alternative candidate was creating Section 34, "How to Actually Follow Through").

## II. What Was Omitted and Why

- **Pomodoro Technique, Timeboxing**: Still lack direct randomized trials. Stated in Section 4, Rule 8 remarks and Section 6 records ("Multitasking / Pomodoro: no direct evidence, omitted per guidelines"). Several trials retrieved treating Pomodoro as a CBT behavioral component are OSF preprints, not journal articles, and thus excluded.
- **"Habit formation averages 66 days"**: This figure appears in the body of Lally 2010, while the abstract only gives the 18 to 254-day range. Under the project rule that figures must be present in the abstract/source verbatim, only the range was written.
- **Effect size of spaced learning**: The abstract of Latimier, Peyre, Ramus (2020, Educational Psychology Review, doi:10.1007/s10648-020-09572-8) was inaccessible across Springer, Semantic Scholar, and Europe PMC (Semantic Scholar notes abstract obscured by publisher). Thus Section 23, Rule 15 omits g values, relying on Cepeda 2006 scale and "longer retention interval requires longer optimal spacing," plus Dunlosky's rating.
- **National Quitline 400-888-5531**: Lacked an official page verifiable word for word (nhc.gov.cn returns 412). The text cites only 12320 and the China Cessation Platform Mini Program, verifiable on the China CDC website.
- **Indications for Acamprosate and Naltrexone in China**: nmpa.gov.cn returns 412; approval documentation could not be retrieved word for word. Thus Section 2, Rule 22 states "follow physician advice and drug insert labels," avoiding assertions on domestic approval.
- **Delirium tremens case fatality rate**: Secondhand claims of "1% to 4%" were rejected in favor of verifiable annual mortality rates from the Norwegian registry cohort (8.0% / 5.0% / 3.6%) and standardized mortality ratio of 9.8.
