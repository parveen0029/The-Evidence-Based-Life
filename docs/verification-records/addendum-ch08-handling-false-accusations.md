# 5 additional articles "After being accused and falsely accused" are added to Section 8 · Verification of records (2026-09-09)

Mission source: Reader’s original words - "Have you ever avoided being falsely accused? Many women in China have falsely accused her of rape. There is a man who has been in prison for several years. He is innocent. It’s useless even if there is evidence. And it’s okay for women to make false accusations casually."

## Dealing with the Reader's Premise

There are three verifiable assertions in the reader's question, which will be dealt with separately this time:

1. **"Many women make false accusations"** - There are no public nationwide statistics on the filing and handling of false accusations. The policy document database of the State Council, the Supreme People's Procuratorate, and the Supreme People's Court cannot find false accusation data by gender or by crime. The rule of this book is that the numbers must be found in the original text, so the main text does not include the incidence rate nor the gender distribution. This is clearly stated in the note in Article 31. Similarly, the case mentioned by the reader cannot be located in a verifiable official notification this time, and the text does not cite the case.
2. **"It's useless even if there is evidence"** - The system level is very clear, what is lacking is implementation rather than provisions: the finalization standard of Article 55 of the Criminal Procedure Law requires the elimination of reasonable doubt, Article 200 (3) stipulates that if the evidence is insufficient, a verdict of not guilty shall be made, Article 56 requires the exclusion of confessions obtained by torture, and Articles 252 and 253 provide channels for appeals and retrials. Therefore, Article 32 was completed, and at the same time, it was stated in the remarks that "these are the bottom lines stated in the system, not a guarantee of results." It was also stated that this book does not have verifiable official data on the acquittal rate and appeal-changed verdict rate of Chinese criminal cases, so the ratio is not included.
3. **"It's okay to make false accusations casually"** - Inaccurate, but the source of the reader's feeling is true. "Public Security Management Punishment Law" (2025 Article 50 (revised in 2016) classifies fabricating facts and making false accusations as a public security offense, while Article 243 of the Criminal Law makes false accusations and frame-ups a crime. However, the third paragraph of the same article states that "if the accusation is not intentional, but a false accusation, or the report is false, the provisions of the first two paragraphs do not apply." - To pursue the other party, you need to prove "fabricated facts" plus "intentionally framed," rather than proving "I have not done it." The difficulty of proving these two things is very different. The text of Article 31 spells out the terms together with this line, without exaggerating or denying it.

## landing point

No new festival. The original Article 5 of Section 8, "When you are accused or summoned, you must first hire a lawyer, and the records will not be deleted or deleted." has covered the time of appointing a lawyer, the length of the summons, the prohibition of forcing self-incrimination, the prohibition of deleting records, as well as the existence of the crime of false accusation and frame-up and state compensation. What is lacking is specific relief beforehand and afterward, so five articles (Articles 29 to 33) are added at the end according to the existing practice of this section, and a sentence pointing to Articles 31, 32, and 33 is added to the remarks of Article 5.

## Sources and pitfalls

- **"Public Security Management Punishment Law" 2025 revised version**: The State Council policy document database only contains the original text of 2005 (this database does not contain the new version of the law enacted by the National People's Congress), and the Supreme People's Procuratorate's legal column does not have it. Finally, we took the full text reprinted from the official website of the Beijing Municipal Public Security Bureau (`gaj.beijing.gov.cn`) and checked the date of revision and adoption, the original text of Rule 50 and the implementation date of Rule 144 January 1, 2026. According to the existing practice of this warehouse, indicate "Reprinted full text" in the source column.
- **Criminal Law Rule 274**: The original text of the Supreme People's Procuratorate's Yes 1997. This Rule has been modified by the "Criminal Law Amendment (8)" (adding "multiple extortion", adding more than ten years and fines), and cannot be copied directly. This Rule follows the integrated text link that has been used in the Rule of Nude Chat and Blackmail in Section 13 (reprinted by the Beijing Municipal Public Security and Traffic Management Bureau). The amount standard is quoted from the original text of Legal Interpretation [2013] No. 10 (direct link to the Supreme Judge website).
- **National Compensation Day Standard**: The release page of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on May 19, 2026 can be directly linked to the verbatim verification to 495.94 yuan. The effective date is May 18, 2026 and the calculation basis. This number is updated in May every year, so the 33rd Rule note states "Check the figure for that year when using it, do not copy the Rule directly."
- **Why the 29th Rule is given a B**: Rule 236 of the Criminal Code only contains the words "other means" and "unable to give valid consent while drunk or comatose." Yes, the judicial general said, this time there is no current public interpretation that can be verified verbatim (the two high and two interpretations for minors do not apply to adults), so the inference from the legal rule to the specific situation is treated as level B, and this boundary is written in the remarks.

## Added: Rule 34 and the Guo Li case (on the same day, a reader submitted "A man was caught claiming milk powder")

The Yes Guo Li case mentioned by readers (the brand Yes "Shi En" involved, not Yes Sanlu itself; both belong to the 2008 melamine incident). This case exposed a blind spot in this round of 30th Rule: The 30th Rule says in Yes "the other party uses the excuse to ask you for money and it will be treated as extortion". Reading it literally will make people think that "the claim price is high = extortion", while the official conclusion of the Guo Li case is exactly the opposite. Therefore, an additional article 34 Rule, and backfill the notes on Rules 30 and 33.

Official original text verified:

- **Guangdong Provincial People's Procuratorate's Retrial Recommendation** (Reprinted by Heyuan City People's Procuratorate, `jcy.heyuan.gov.cn/node/142`; Guangdong Provincial Procuratorate's original site `gd.jcy.gov.cn` The local TLS certificate verification failed, so the reprinted page is cited and noted in the source column): The examination result of Guo Li's daughter "several dot-like strong echoes can be seen in the central collecting system of both kidneys"; 2009 In June 2016, the two parties reached an agreement, Shi En Company compensated 400,000 yuan, and Guo Li issued a written document stating that he would no longer pursue the case and gave up the compensation request; after that, the company reported the case, and the Chao'an County People's Court sentenced him to five years in prison for extortion. The three sentences of the prosecutor's reasons were introduced verbatim into the text: "The existing evidence is not enough to prove that Guo Li subjectively had the purpose of illegal possession." "He has the right to claim compensation from the milk powder manufacturer, and his claim behavior has a legal basis." "Have the right to expose and criticize through the mass media." On April 7, 2017, the Guangdong Provincial Higher People's Court acquitted him in a retrial.
- **State Compensation Decision** (Guangdong Court Website Judgment Selection, Guangdong Provincial Higher People’s Court Compensation Committee (2018) Guangdong Commission Compensation No. 11): The original sentence of the reason for the change of judgment in the retrial was "The original trial found that Guo Li used threats and threats to forcibly obtain property for the purpose of illegal possession. The facts were unclear and the evidence was insufficient. The nature of Guo Li's behavior did not exceed the scope of civil disputes."; Detention in July 2009 23 to July 22, 2014, a total of 1,826 days; based on the national average daily wage of employees in 2016 of 258.89 yuan, the personal freedom compensation is 472,733.14 yuan; the mental damage consolation payment is 165,450 yuan; the ten-year mental damage consolation payment proposed by Guo Li and others will not be supported. Compensation for the right to life and health, property losses, attorney fees, detention center living allowances, etc. were determined to be outside the statutory scope of state compensation.

Writing decisions:

- **Rule 34 is given to B**, because it is a single case but not a generalizable ratio. It proves two things at the same time - the claim itself is legal, and it is still possible to be filed - this is stated in the text and notes, and "not guilty" is not written as an inevitable result.
- **Two details in the reader's report were not included in the text**: 1. Yes, "The annual salary is hundreds of thousands, the leg is lame, and the wife is gone." There is no record of the personal situation in the official documents that can be verified verbatim; 2. Yes, "Sanlu asked him to go over, but he was arrested in the past." Reporting and filing a case usually takes place at the location of the other party. This is an actionable reminder, so I won’t go into detail in this Section.
- **The 33rd Rule note supplements the actual calculation of this case**, because it explains two things that are easily misunderstood: the Japanese standard uses the annual standard applicable when making compensation decisions (the detention ended in 2014, but used the 258.89 yuan in 2016), and the legal scope of state compensation does not include lost wages, attorney fees and property losses.
