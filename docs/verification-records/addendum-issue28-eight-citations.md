## Grading

**Only two rules had their grades updated: Section 13, Rule 18 and Section 20, Rule 9, both upgraded from Grade C to Grade B.** Both rules are now backed by official guidelines or professional consensus alongside supporting research, but neither has figures directly convertible into "how many fewer deaths if executed," which qualifies as Grade B under project metrics.

All other rules keep their existing grades. Operational guidelines from official agencies (NPS, CISA, FCC) do not constitute scientific research and remain Grade C per project conventions (as has always been the case for Section 13, Rule 27). The two rules in Sections 4 and 5 merely removed TODOs and supplied missing figures, keeping grades untouched.
