## 3. Methodological Scope and Benefit Magnitudes

- **Wild Mushrooms and Gas Safety rated "Mortality, High":** The intervention completely eliminates a lethal exposure pathway rather than offering a minor percentage risk reduction.
- **Compulsory Auto Insurance rated "Money, High" and is one of the book's rare "Money=High" entries (commercial third-party premiums cost thousands annually):** It shields against extreme tail risk, as liabilities exceeding mandatory statutory limits have zero public backstop.
- **Prepaid Memberships, Live-Stream Commerce, Personal Insurance Sharing, and Minor Recharging rated "Money, Med":** Monetary exposure ranges from hundreds to tens of thousands of RMB.
- **Dog Leashing Liability rated "Money, High":** Medical treatment, rabies vaccines, immunoglobulins, lost wages, and civil settlements frequently exceed 10,000 RMB, with substantially higher liabilities if children are injured.
- **Minor Recharging rated Time Cost "Med":** The actual time cost involves preserving digital forensic evidence and negotiating with platform arbitration departments, not merely filing the initial complaint.
