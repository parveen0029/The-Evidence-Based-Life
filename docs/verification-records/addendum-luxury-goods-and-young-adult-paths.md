## Trade-off Explanations

- **Did not conclude "luxury goods do not hold value"**: China has no citable official secondhand transaction statistics; listing prices on resale platforms are not realized transaction prices. Section 5, Rule 33 only records verifiable hard numbers on both sides: consumption tax in purchase price, and statutory pawn rate caps and forfeiture rules for liquidation, noting in remarks that "50% loss in one year" is a rough annualized multiplication of monthly rates.
- **Neutral stance on tastes and motives**: If you like it, buy it as pure consumption; what the book cautions against is solely the rationalization of "it preserves value / can be sold for more later." Section 6, Rule 21 explicitly avoids judging cultural or religious customs, evaluating solely "verifiable efficacy and predictive validity."
- **Section 6, Rule 22 marked with controversy**: Included studies are cross-sectional correlations; authors themselves note the need for experimental, longitudinal research; r = -0.19 explains only a small percentage of variance; both samples are Western, lacking Chinese population data. Documented in remarks, graded B rather than A.
- **Section 31 expanded as a "Pathways Map", not overloaded with military enlistment**: Added three rules covering government-funded normal/medical students, overseas labor, and flexible/platform employment. Section intro specifies boundaries: entering state-owned enterprises, factories, seafaring, or freelancing lack unified public national guidelines, so requirements are not assumed.
- **Excluded "Selected Candidates (Xuandiaosheng)"**: Central Organization Department has no open national regulations verifiable word for word; provincial notices vary widely.
- **Entrepreneurship guaranteed loans placed in Section 31 rather than Section 12**: Section 12, Rule 1 warns "only start a business with money you can afford to lose; do not borrow to start." This rule covers "if you have already decided to borrow, which tier has the lowest cost," serving as an available resource without contradicting Section 12. The 2020 pandemic-era 200,000-yuan cap notice was temporary; current policy follows Caijin [2023] No. 75.
