# Addendum: Enforcement & Dishonest Judgment Debtors, Loan Fraud via Falsified Documents, and Substances Offered by Strangers · Verification Record (2026-09-08)

Task Origin: A reader raised four specific matters: What to do after being placed on the dishonest judgment debtor list; whether viral social media tactics for evading judicial enforcement—such as "transferring all funds and property to relatives while registering the residential villa under a corporate name as staff dormitories"—are legally viable; organized syndicates fabricating paperwork to obtain bank loans ("arbitraging / taking the money") and absconding abroad; and precautions against synthetic new psychoactive substances (not eating candy from strangers, never drinking beverages left unattended in public venues).

Original Coverage: Section 7 Rule 20 only addressed the duration and removal of dishonest judgment debtor records (a fresh start pathway), omitting what to do and avoid during active enforcement; Section 8 Rule 12 only covered loan IOUs and guarantees; Section 9 Rule 4 covered assisting cash withdrawals and transfers (two-card schemes), without loan fraud; Section 22 Rule 3 advised leaving entertainment venues immediately if handed suspicious items, lacking the side of being deceived into consuming spiked substances. All four topics are non-duplicative, landing across three sections: Section 8 added 2 rules (Rules 12 and 13); Section 9 added 1 rule (Rule 5); Section 22 added 1 rule (Rule 4). Original rules from Section 8 Rule 13 onward, Section 9 Rule 5 onward, and Section 22 Rule 4 onward shifted accordingly.
