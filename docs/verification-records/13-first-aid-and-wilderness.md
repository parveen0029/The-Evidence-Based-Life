## 3. Caliber and revenue magnitude

Seven of the nine criteria are mortality (including health endpoints). The magnitude of the income is determined according to the general standards of Section 13: those that directly determine life or death (anaphylaxis, severe hypoglycemia, electric shock, carbon monoxide, lightning strikes, high altitude cerebral edema/pulmonary edema) are rated as "large"; those that mainly avoid secondary injury or delay (epilepsy, ticks, drinking water in the wild) are rated as "medium". Except for ticks (you need a pair of pointed tweezers, money = little), all costs are zero. Lightning strikes and plateaus require extra "perseverance = some" - the difficulty is not knowing, but being willing to turn around when thunder strikes, and being willing to descend on plateaus.
