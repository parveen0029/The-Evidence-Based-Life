## 5. "Labor Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Law" (Presidential Order No. 80)
- URL: <https://chinajob.mohrss.gov.cn/h5/c/2022-07-15/356212.shtml> (Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security China Employment Network)
- Status: Open successfully.
- Article 27: "The statute of limitations for applying for arbitration of labor disputes is one year. ... If a dispute arises due to arrears of labor remuneration during the existence of the labor relationship, the employee's application for arbitration is not subject to the limitation of the arbitration statute of limitations stipulated in paragraph 1 of this article; however, if the labor relationship is terminated, it must be submitted within one year from the date of termination of the labor relationship."
- Article 43: "The arbitral tribunal's decision on a labor dispute case shall be concluded within forty-five days from the date when the labor dispute arbitration committee accepts the arbitration application. ... The extension period shall not exceed fifteen days."
- Article 44: "In cases involving recovery of labor remuneration, work-related injury medical expenses, economic compensation or damages, the arbitral tribunal may, based on the application of the parties, make an order for enforcement first and transfer it to the People's Court for enforcement."
- Article 47: "Disputes over the recovery of labor remuneration, medical expenses for work-related injuries, economic compensation or compensation, not exceeding the local monthly minimum wage standard for twelve months" are final.
- Articles 48 and 49: If an employee is dissatisfied with the final ruling, he or she may sue within 15 days; the employer can only apply to the Intermediate Court for revocation within 30 days.
- Article 50: "If a party is dissatisfied with the arbitration award in other labor dispute cases other than those provided for in Article 47 of this Law, it may file a lawsuit with the People's Court within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the arbitration award."
- Article 53: "There is no charge for labor dispute arbitration. The funds of the Labor Dispute Arbitration Committee shall be guaranteed by the finance."

## 7. "Civil Procedure Law" (2023 amendment)
- Preferred URL failed: <https://www.ssf.gov.cn/...pdf> (certificate error); China National People's Congress website did not find a full-text page that could be opened directly.
- Actual URL: <https://amr.guizhou.gov.cn/zwgk/xxgkml/jcxxgk/zcfg/fl/202401/t20240129_83645867.html> (Reprinted by Guizhou Provincial Market Supervision Bureau, gov.cn domain)
- Status: Open successfully.
- Article 109: "The people's court may rule on the following cases, based on the application of the parties, to grant execution first: (2) Recovering labor remuneration."
- Article 152: "Cases tried by the People's Court applying ordinary procedures shall be concluded within six months from the date of filing the case. ... This extension may be extended by six months."
- Article 164: "The people's court applying simplified procedures to cases heard shall conclude the case within three months from the date of filing. ... It may be extended by one month."
- Article 183: "When the People's Court hears an appeal case against a judgment, it shall conclude the case within three months from the date of filing of the second instance case."
- Article 225: "A creditor who requests the debtor to pay money or securities and meets the following conditions may apply to the grassroots people's court with jurisdiction for a payment order: (1) The creditor and the debtor have no other debt disputes; (2) The payment order can be served on the debtor."
- Article 227: "After accepting the application, the people's court shall, after reviewing the facts and evidence provided by the creditor, find that the relationship between the creditor and the debt is clear and legal, and shall issue a payment order to the debtor within fifteen days from the date of acceptance."
- Article 228: If the debtor's written objection is established, "the ruling terminates the supervision procedure and the payment order will automatically become invalid" - the article therefore limits the payment order to situations where "there are IOUs or salary schedules and the facts are clear".

## 8. Supreme People’s Court Interpretation [2013] No. 3
- Preferred URL failed: <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu-xiangqing-5459.html> (404), <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/9cc6f93c9e7a7a3d2c93009a581a7b.html> (502).
- Actual URL: <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/5041.html>
- Status: Open successfully. Law Interpretation [2013] No. 3, passed on 2013-01-14 and implemented on 2013-01-23.
- Article 3: "Anyone who falls under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed to be a "relatively large amount" as stipulated in the first paragraph of Article 276-1 of the Criminal Law: (1) Refusal to pay labor remuneration to one worker for more than three months and the amount is more than 5,000 yuan to 20,000 yuan; (2) Refusal to pay labor remuneration to more than ten workers and the cumulative amount is more than 30,000 yuan to 100,000 yuan." Each provincial high court can set local standards within the range.

## 10. "Regulations on Guaranteeing Wage Payment for Migrant Workers" (State Council Order No. 724)
- URL：<https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5469641.htm>
- Status: Open successfully. Implemented on 2020-05-01.
- Article 10: "Migrant workers who are owed wages have the right to complain in accordance with the law, or apply for labor dispute mediation and arbitration or file lawsuits. Any unit or individual has the right to report any behavior that arrears wages to migrant workers to the human resources and social security administrative department or other relevant departments."
- Article 24: "...The payment cycle for labor costs shall not exceed one month."
- Article 26: "The construction general contractor shall open a special account for the wages of migrant workers in accordance with relevant regulations, which shall be used exclusively to pay the wages of migrant workers in the construction project."
- Article 30: "If a subcontracting unit defaults on wages to migrant workers, the construction general contractor shall pay off the wages first and then pursue compensation in accordance with the law."
- Article 31: "In the field of engineering construction, the system of entrusting the construction general contractor to pay the wages of migrant workers in subcontracting units shall be implemented... wages will be paid directly to the bank accounts of migrant workers through special accounts for migrant workers' wages."
- Article 32: "The construction general contractor shall store salary deposits in accordance with relevant regulations..."
- Article 36: If a contract is issued to an individual or an unqualified unit, resulting in wage arrears, "the construction unit or general construction contracting unit shall repay it."
- Article 41: Those suspected of refusing to pay labor remuneration "should be promptly transferred to the public security organs for review and decision in accordance with relevant regulations."
