## Failure to adopt the conclusion of the issue

**Do not write "Don't speculate in A shares". **According to CLAUDE.md's two clauses: "Only dismantle the account but not provide a conclusion" and "Confirm whether the person concerned really has no choice before judging a situation that only prohibits and does not provide a way out": Article 17 already provides a low-cost method of "taking money into the stock market" (broad-based index funds), and writing another "Don't buy it at all" will directly conflict with it. So these two articles are about differences in action - the same money, the same market, how to determine the selling rules, how much to do when the market is the hottest, rather than "enter or not to enter the market."

**Do not write "90% of retail investors lose money". ** This number is widely circulated on the Internet, but no official statistics can be found that can be verified verbatim: the Shanghai Stock Exchange and Shenzhen Stock Exchange’s statistical yearbooks and investor status survey reports do not provide a public time series for the "proportion of loss-making accounts", and no original documents can be cited this time. According to the project rules, "I would rather not write an uncertain number." Note 38 clearly states that this number is not used, and provides a verifiable replacement - the loss amount of the smallest 85% of the accounts in the same batch of accounts that "has been held unchanged".

** "It's almost impossible to figure it out in one year" was not written. ** This is a question of return time. The holding period income needs to be calculated based on the buying point distribution. There is no original calculation that can be cited in this round, so I am not writing based on my impression.

## Rating and magnitude

Both are not meta-analyses, but according to the project caliber "large cohort + quantifiable numbers", they are A-level.

**The income in Article 37 is determined as "medium". ** The monetary caliber is based on the amount threshold: the verifiable amount is "3.4 percentage points higher in the next year" and 4.4% in the calculation example. For an account with a level of 100,000 yuan, it is several thousand yuan a year, which falls into the "hundreds to thousands" category. "Large" is not specified because the original article does not provide a cumulative amount; Article 15 is "large" because that article gives an annualized long-term gap of 6.5 percentage points.

**Article 38 The income is determined to be "large". ** The original article gives "equivalent to 28% of the market value of the stocks at the beginning of this group." A 100,000-yuan account corresponds to a 10,000-yuan loss, which falls into the "10,000-yuan level".

**Perseverance must be "some". ** Consistent with items 15 and 16 in the same cluster: What needs to be changed in these two items is a decision-making habit, not a long-term inertia that must be fought every day.

## Three decisions about writing

**Article 37 is half right when it comes to recognizing the "amortized cost of covering positions". ** Directly saying "covering a position is wrong" will be refuted by readers as an arithmetic error (buying more does lower the average cost). The remark is written as "arithmetically, it is correct, but the mistake is to regard it as a way to turn around", and then point out that its real effect is to increase the position of a single target, and go to Article 19.

**The tax deduction section indicates the US tax system. ** 4.4% includes tax deductions for early realization of losses, which is not available in China. The remark only says "China does not copy it, the rest still holds true" and does not mention the details of China's tax system - that requires separately citing fiscal and taxation documents, which is not the topic of this article.

**The term "loss" in Article 38 is written in the main text. ** The 250B in the original article is the active trading profit and loss relative to buy-and-hold, not the floating loss on the book. There is a big difference between the two. The income column specifically adds a sentence, "The 'loss' here refers to holding it unchanged." Otherwise, readers will regard it as "a loss of 250 billion."
