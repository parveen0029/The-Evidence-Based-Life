Graded B: Official sources provide technical detection heuristics and law enforcement case figures, without trial effect sizes demonstrating percentage drops in fraud victimization following education. Benefit magnitude rated "large"—dimension is money; wire fraud impersonating acquaintances and executives routinely causes losses in the tens of thousands of yuan or more per occurrence, meeting the "large" threshold; Costs: Money = 0, Time = low, Willpower = some (overcoming the intuitive bias of "I saw their face on video" to place a separate verification call), synthesizing to "high" cost-effectiveness.

Unretrieved: MPS official portal mps.gov.cn returned sitewide HTTP 521 in this round; materials from MPS Cybersecurity and Criminal Investigation bureaus were retrieved from Fujian Provincial PSD mirrors, cited with "reprint" transparently noted in source columns.
