## An outdated reference that was fixed incidentally

Former Rule 7 (now Rule 9) "Invoices are issued only as real transactions" quoted in Yes Announcement of the State Administration of Taxation of the Ministry of Finance No. 19 of 2023 "Monthly sales of less than 100,000 yuan are exempt from VAT". After verification, the VAT law came into force on January 01, 2026, and the starting point was changed to be stipulated by the State Council: Announcement No. 10, 2026, First Rule of the State Administration of Taxation of the Ministry of Finance "From January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2027... (1) If a month is used as a tax period, the starting point shall be 100,000 yuan in monthly sales. With a quarter as a tax period, the starting point is 300,000 yuan of quarterly sales. (2) If the tax is paid on a one-time basis, the starting point is 1,000 yuan per sale (day)"; the sixth rule "The preferential VAT policy of the domestic ring section stipulated in the document issued before December 31, 2025 shall be stopped at the same time"; the supporting State Administration of Taxation Announcement No. 4 of 2026 has explicitly abolished Announcement No. 1 of 2023. The threshold of the amount has not changed. According to the full replacement, the Rule item has been rewritten in accordance with Rule 23 of the VAT Law and No. 10 of 2026.
