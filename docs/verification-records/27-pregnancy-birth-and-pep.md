Contents and reasons not included in the text:

- **"If a newborn is insured within 90 days of birth, you can enjoy benefits from the date of birth" do not specify the specific number of days**. This article has been widely circulated, but this time there is no unified national statement that can be verified verbatim in the public documents of the Chinese government website and the National Medical Insurance Administration. What is available is only the statement from the 2024 briefing that "you can enroll in insurance online with a birth medical certificate, and you can be reimbursed after you leave the hospital." Therefore, only the parts that can be verified should be written in the main text, and it should be stated in the remarks that the number of days varies from place to place and should be subject to the local medical insurance department.
- **Specific grading of the five-color management of maternal pregnancy risk assessment**. "Working Standards for Maternal Pregnancy Risk Assessment and Management" has not been included in the policy document library of the State Council (`searchfield=title` has still not been hit in the review), and the original text that can be verified verbatim is not available. The text only states the qualitative statement "the sooner the registration is established, the sooner the pregnancy risk classification will be made", without the color and corresponding treatment.
- **National popularity rate of painless delivery and Chinese figures for cesarean section rate**. No official annual statistics were obtained that could be verified verbatim, and the text was not written.
- **Specific ways to count fetal movements (how many times per hour, how many times per 12 hours)**. The formal fetal movement counting program has not shown the effect of reducing deaths in large-scale randomized trials. It is not the same thing as "seeking medical attention if fetal movement is significantly reduced." The text only retains the latter (incorporated into Article 7 of the CDC list) without counting the caliber.
- Article 8 defines B instead of A: The popular science page of the Beijing Municipal Health Commission provides treatment actions but does not explain the mechanism of umbilical cord prolapse. The mechanism sentence is taken from Beijing Shunyi District Maternal and Child Health Hospital (the official website of a public medical institution). Both materials are not guidelines or meta-analyses.
- The magnitude of the benefit in Article 10 is "medium" rather than "large": the WHO statement gives a negative conclusion of "no further improvement after exceeding 10%", not the effect size of "how many deaths can be reduced by not doing non-medically indicated cesarean section".

Section 13, Article 37, determines B instead of A: The time limit and treatment course come from the official popular science and technical guidelines (trial), not a randomized trial; PEP itself does not and cannot have a placebo-controlled trial. Follow-up time points (weeks 4 to 6, 3 months) are written in notes and noted as per physician's orders and are not labeled as verified official numbers.

Section 1, Item 28 Cost Record "Perseverance = Some": The difficulty lies in using it every time rather than spending money. Article 29 Cost Record "Money = 0 Perseverance = Some": The regulations clearly indicate that it is free, and perseverance means "willingness to check." Section 13, Article 37, Cost Record: "Money=More": A course of treatment costs thousands of yuan, and most areas pay for it themselves. This round does not have unified national fees or reimbursement regulations. The text is written as "most areas pay for themselves" and does not give a caliber outside the specific amount range.
